One programme. Three stages.

More than a week in Geneva

IPP is a structured international learning journey combining virtual collaboration, practical project development and immersive exposure to Geneva's international ecosystem.

01

Explore the challenge

Participants examine global challenges through the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and identify an issue they want to address.

02

Develop the solution

Working in international teams, participants research, structure and strengthen an impact-focused project through guided collaboration and mentoring.

03

Experience Geneva

The international cohort comes together in Geneva for expert-led sessions, leadership challenges, institutional learning visits, project presentations and recognition.

04
Why Geneva

Learn where global ideas become global action

Geneva is one of the world's most important centres for diplomacy, humanitarian action, scientific cooperation and international leadership. IPP transforms the city and its surrounding institutional ecosystem into a living classroom.

More international organisations are headquartered in Geneva than in any other city in the world. Diplomacy, humanitarian action, global health, labour standards, trade and fundamental science all operate here, within a few kilometres of one another — which is why a single week on the ground can cover ground a semester of reading cannot.

Over the Geneva Week, participants move between five institutions, each representing a different model of international cooperation.

InstitutionWhat participants explore
Palais des NationsMultilateral diplomacy, negotiation and international cooperation in practice
International Committee of the Red CrossHumanitarian action, crisis response and the protection of human dignity
CERNCross-border scientific collaboration, knowledge and innovation
Patek Philippe MuseumHeritage, precision and long-term excellence in Swiss tradition
Olympic Museum & the Olympic Movement, LausanneSport as a platform for shared values, cooperation and social impact
Together, these experiences introduce participants to different models of global leadership — from diplomacy and humanitarian action to science, enterprise and sport.

What participants develop

Multilateral understanding

Project leadership

Cross-cultural collaboration

06
The Geneva learning journey

Seven days of learning, collaboration and global exposure

The Geneva Week is organised around learning themes rather than conventional sightseeing, with each session and institutional experience contributing to the participant journey.

SUN 29 NOV1

Arrival, Orientation and Global Cohort Connection

Arrive in Geneva, connect with fellow participants and prepare for the week through programme orientation and international cohort-building activities.

MON 30 NOV2

Inside Multilateral Diplomacy: The United Nations, Global Cooperation and International Careers

Begin the Geneva experience with an official welcome, expert-led sessions and exposure to the Palais des Nations. Explore how multilateral institutions operate and discover possible academic and professional pathways within the international ecosystem.

TUE 1 DEC3

Leadership in Crisis, the Entrepreneurship Odyssey and Collaborative Decision-Making

Take part in immersive challenges designed to strengthen resilience, adaptability, entrepreneurial thinking and teamwork, followed by an evening reflection cruise on Lake Geneva.

WED 2 DEC4

Global Institutions in Action: Diplomacy, Humanitarian Leadership and Science

Examine how major international institutions respond to global challenges through learning experiences connected to the Palais des Nations, the International Committee of the Red Cross and CERN.

THU 3 DEC5

The Olympic Movement, Global Values and Leadership Through Sport

Travel to Lausanne to explore the role of the Olympic Movement, international sport and shared values in promoting cooperation, inclusion and positive social impact.

FRI 4 DEC6

From Project to Impact: Final Presentations, Recognition and Reflection

Bring together the full IPP journey through concluding sessions, participant presentations, the awards ceremony and structured reflection at UNITAR.

SAT 5 DEC7

Departure from Geneva — Continuing the Journey

Depart with new capabilities, meaningful international connections and a deeper understanding of how ideas can be transformed into global impact.

Frequently asked questions

Before you apply

Who can apply to IPP?

IPP is designed for university students and emerging leaders seeking international exposure and practical experience in diplomacy, leadership, entrepreneurship and global impact.

Is IPP limited to students from international relations or political science?

No. IPP welcomes participants from all academic disciplines, including business, engineering, technology, law, medicine, social sciences and the humanities.

How is the programme delivered?

IPP begins with a two-week guided remote project phase. Participants then travel to Geneva for the final seven-day immersive learning experience.

Is visa support provided?

Successful participants receive relevant programme documentation to support their visa application. Visa decisions remain under the authority of the relevant embassy or consulate.

Can universities nominate a delegation?

Yes. Universities and educational institutions may nominate groups of students to participate as part of the international cohort.
