About the programme

The Academic
Leadership Suite

A boutique, UN-certified executive programme delivered at the United Nations in Geneva in partnership with UNITAR and Ureka Education Group.

The programme

A four-day immersion at the UN

ALS is not a conference. It is a structured, cohort-based executive programme combining expert-led sessions, institutional visits, and peer dialogue — all inside the United Nations in Geneva.

Delivered as part of Global Education Week at UNITAR Geneva, ALS positions participants within a broader international platform for high-level dialogue, policy engagement, and innovation in education.

Geneva, Switzerland

Your Geneva
experience

The diplomatic capital of the world. Here is everything you need to navigate it comfortably.

July in Geneva
Warm (22–27°C), long evenings, the lake is beautiful. Occasional summer storms — bring a compact umbrella. The city is very walkable and the UN area is a 15-minute tram ride from the centre.
Getting around

Transport in Geneva

From the airport (GVA)
Airport transfers are not included in the ALS fee. From Geneva Airport: free 80-min transit ticket at baggage claim. Train R to Gare Cornavin: 7 min, every 10 min. Taxi: ~20 min, CHF 35–45. Participants arrange their own transfer.
Tram & bus
Lines 15 and F serve the UN area. Hotels in the canton of Geneva include a complimentary public transport pass.
Walking
City centre to Palais des Nations: ~25 min along the lake. Highly recommended in July.
Taxi & Uber
Uber operates in Geneva. City centre to UN: CHF 15–25. TaxiPhone for metered taxis. A car is not necessary.
Accommodation — not included
Accommodation is always excluded from the ALS fee because participants have different budgets, hotel standards, room preferences, and lengths of stay. There is no official ALS hotel. Stay anywhere in Geneva with convenient public transport access to the UN, WMO, and WHO areas. Hotels in the canton of Geneva typically include a complimentary Geneva public transport pass for the duration of your stay — making daily travel to programme venues easy and free. Book as soon as your participation is confirmed.
Practicalities

Day-to-day in Geneva

Swiss Franc (CHF)
Switzerland uses CHF, not euros. Card payments widely accepted. Budget CHF 25–40 for a restaurant lunch. 1 USD ≈ 0.88 CHF.
July weather
22–27°C daytime, 15–17°C evenings. Occasional summer storms. Light layers for the cruise. Sunscreen recommended.
Power & connectivity
Switzerland uses Type J plugs (230V). Bring an adapter. Hotel WiFi is reliable. Swiss SIM cards at the airport CHF 10–20.
Language
Geneva is French-speaking, but English is widely spoken in all professional and hospitality settings.

Financial grant

The Ureka Financial Grant

Grant amount
USD 1,500 reduction applied to the programme fee. October 2026 also offers a USD 500 early bird discount for payment by 31 July 2026. Combined, the total can be as low as USD 4,000.

The grant is merit-based — not available to everyone. To be considered, submit a Statement of Purpose (max 600 words):
