## 4. 2027 pipeline strategy (the real plan)

### 4.1 LinkedIn ABM — the primary channel
- **Organic + founder-network DMs first** (free; Ureka has never run LinkedIn ads — library empty).
  Wael's 5K-follower company page + Geneva CEGI think-tank page give institutional credibility.
- Content engine: UNITAR co-branded proof (Sports Diplomacy White Paper model shows Ureka can
  co-publish with UNITAR — an **Academic Leadership White Paper** would be the perfect ALS lead
  magnet for deans).
- LinkedIn Ads (later, if organic stalls): title targeting = Dean, Provost, Vice-Chancellor,
  Pro-VC, Director of Internationalization; geo US/UAE/QA/GCC; lead-gen forms with the grant
  ($1,500 merit) as the offer. Budget only after IPP test proves tracking end-to-end.
- Expect LinkedIn CPLs of $50–150 for this seniority — inside the $54 target only at the top of
  that range's luck; judge on **pipeline quality**, not CPL (a single enrolment = $4,500).

### 4.2 Search — brand + micro-defensive only (~$5/day, shared brand campaign)
- `ureka als`, `academic leadership summit geneva`, `higher education leadership program` (exact).
- Judged on brand protection. Never on volume — A2 stands: at 70/mo it cannot be a growth channel.

### 4.3 The IPP↔ALS flywheel
Every IPP delegation conversation reaches the exact ALS buyer:
- IPP delegation contact = Internationalization Office / dean → **ALS prospect themselves**
- ALS attendee (dean) → next year's IPP delegation sponsor
- The `/delegations/` page and outreach deck should carry an ALS cross-sell block, and the ALS
  page a "bring a student delegation to IPP" block.
One outreach list, two revenue lines. Track separately (own conversion events).
