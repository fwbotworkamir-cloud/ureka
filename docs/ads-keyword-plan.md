## 4. Honest caveats

1. **Season mismatch.** The big terms say "summer"; IPP's Geneva week is 29 Nov–5 Dec.
   Bidding summer terms in Aug–Sept catches families planning *summer 2027* — usable as
   2027 pipeline, not for the 4 Oct deadline. The terms that fit the November cohort are the
   non-seasonal ones: `leadership programs…`, `pre college programs`, `study abroad…`,
   `exchange programs…`, `youth leadership program` (~3,500/mo aggregate US).
2. **"High school students" = mostly minors.** The searcher is usually the parent — good
   (they pay) — but ad copy targets parents/18+, per audit A4.
3. **Term-time clash (A10) survives.** Search can deliver the lead; the page must still answer
   "miss a week of school in December?" head-on.
4. **CPC data is directional** at these volumes; expect real CPCs 1.5–2× Ahrefs.
